Trailhead

Design system

Tokens, base styles and components for the half-marathon tracker. Burgundy surfaces, cream text, gold for Beatrix (person A) and olive for Julian (person B). Mobile first, Strava-like: cards, a bottom tab bar, bottom sheets. Hover, press and focus states are live: interact with anything below. Documentation: docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

Colour

Components use only the semantic tokens. Primitives are the raw ramps they are built from.
